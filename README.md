# The Life of Daniel

A storyline timeline Bible study of the book of Daniel, from Hezekiah's treasures to Daniel 12, in English and simple Egyptian Arabic.

Live at https://daniel.kamelnabil.com

- Story bullets restate the Scripture text; history and interpretation sit in labelled boxes.
- Verse tooltips show the Arabic Smith & Van Dyke text (public domain). NLT passages are links to Bible.com.
- Single self-contained `index.html`, deployed on Vercel.
